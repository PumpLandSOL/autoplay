// how it works
Three moves. Zero code.
01 · patch
Wire the rules
Drag cables from triggers (fees arrive, every 6h, mcap passes $100k) to actions (buy back, burn, airdrop, buy TSLA). Or say it in one line.
02 · launch
One signature
Your wallet funds the coin's vault. The memo carries the patch's SHA-256. The vault creates the coin on pump.fun as its creator.
03 · it plays
Forever, on its fees
Every 10 minutes the vault claims its creator fees and plays the patch. Every move is a Solana transaction with a receipt.
