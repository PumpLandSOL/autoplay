Fingerprint on-chain, twice

The creator's funding tx and the vault's own memo both carry the SHA-256 of the patch. The verify page recomputes it in your browser.

Fees claimed only when it pays

The vault simulates the fee claim for free first and only sends it once there's enough to beat the gas.

Provably fair random holder

The winner is picked weighted by balance from the run's Solana blockhash. The seed is in the receipt; anyone can recompute it.

Real stocks in the vault

"Buy a stock" swaps SOL for xStocks (TSLAx, NVDAx, SPYx) through Jupiter, and the vault holds them.
