Open the bench →How it works

Three moves.

No code, no contracts to deploy, no trust in a team: the rules are fixed at launch and their fingerprint is on Solana.

01 · patchWire your rules

Drag cables from triggers (fees arrive, every 6h, market cap passes $100k, price dumps 20%) to actions (buy back, burn, airdrop, random holder, buy a stock, pay creator). Or type one sentence.

02 · launchOne signature

Your wallet funds the coin's vault, and the memo carries the patch's SHA-256. The vault creates the coin on pump.fun as its creator and makes the first buy.

03 · it playsForever, on its fees

Every few minutes the vault checks its creator fees, claims them when they're worth claiming, and runs your patch. Every action is a Solana transaction you can click.

Recipes.

Built to be checked.

The coin's patch is public, its vault address is public, and every action carries a transaction signature.

#
Fingerprint on-chain, twice

The creator's funding transaction and the vault's own memo both carry the SHA-256 of the canonical patch. The verify page recomputes it in your browser.

◎
Fees claimed only when it pays

The vault simulates the creator-fee claim for free first and only sends it once there's enough to beat the gas. No vault bleeding on empty claims.

⚄
Provably fair random holder

The winner is picked weighted by balance from the run's Solana blockhash. The receipt carries the seed, so anyone can recompute the pick.

▤
Real stocks, not wrappers we made

"Buy a stock" swaps SOL for Backed's xStocks (TSLAx, NVDAx, SPYx) through Jupiter, and the vault holds them.

⏱
Timers and market triggers

Not just "when fees arrive": every N hours, market cap milestones, pump and dump reactions, each with its own budget from the vault.

✓
Receipts for everything

Claims, buybacks, burns, airdrops, failures too. Nothing is summarized away; each row links to Solscan.
