The whole machine.

Overview

A PATCHBAY coin is a pump.fun coin whose creator is a vault, not a person. pump.fun pays creator fees on every trade to the creator, so the coin's trading fees land in the vault, and the vault spends them exactly the way the coin's patch says: buy back, burn, airdrop holders, pay a random holder, buy tokenized stocks, pay the creator, or keep it for later.

A patch is fixed at launch. There is no edit endpoint, and its SHA-256 fingerprint is written on Solana twice, so anyone can check that the rules on the site are the rules the coin launched with.

Builder /build

The bench is a patch editor. Triggers sit on the top row with an OUT jack; actions sit on the bottom row with an IN jack. Drag from an OUT jack to an IN jack to plug a cable. The number on each cable is that action's share of the trigger's SOL budget; click it to change it. Click a cable to unplug it.

  • The readout describes the patch in plain English.
  • The split bar shows exactly where 1 SOL of creator fees would go.
  • The fingerprint is computed by the server and recomputed in your browser; the two must match before you can launch.

Rules the bench enforces: 1–6 triggers, 1–10 actions, up to 24 cables, cables out of one trigger add up to at most 100%, and every action needs at least one cable.

Quick launch

Type what the coin should do in one sentence, on the home page or the bench, and press Patch it. The sentence is turned into modules and cables you can then adjust. It understands buy back, burn, airdrop (with "top N"), random holder, Tesla / Nvidia / S&P 500, pay the creator, keep, "every N hours", "when market cap passes $X", "price jumps / drops X%", percentages, "half", "a third" and "the rest".

buy back 60% and burn, 20% to the creator, keep the rest
When fees come in, buy Tesla with half and airdrop the rest to holders.
Every hour, send 20% of the vault to one random holder.

Flywheel

  1. Trading on pump.fun (or PumpSwap after graduation) pays creator fees.
  2. The vault is the creator, so those fees accrue to it.
  3. The executor claims them when the claim is worth more than its gas.
  4. The patch spends them: buybacks put buy pressure on the coin and the burn removes supply; airdrops and random-holder payouts reward holders; stock buys build a treasury the coin keeps.
  5. More holders and volume mean more fees, and the loop turns again.

Examples

The recipes on the home page load straight onto the bench. A few patterns that work well:

  • Pure deflation: fees → buy back 100% → burn 100% of held.
  • Dividend coin: fees → airdrop 80% to the top 20, keep 20% for timers.
  • Treasury coin: fees → buy TSLA 50%, airdrop 50%.
  • Event coin: market cap passes $1M → burn 50% of held; price drops 25% → buy back with 30% of the vault.

Stock rules

The Buy a stock action swaps SOL for xStocks, tokenized US equities issued by Backed and traded on Solana, routed through Jupiter with 1.5% max slippage. Supported: TSLA (XsDoVf…JzoB), NVDA (Xsc9qv…9qEh), SPY (XsoCS1…DF2W). The vault keeps what it buys; holdings are read live from the chain on the coin page.

Launching

  1. Patch on the bench, or start from a sentence.
  2. Metadata: name, ticker, image and links are published to pump.fun's IPFS. If that endpoint is unavailable, PATCHBAY serves the standard metadata JSON itself at /meta/<id>.json, which pump.fun reads the same way.
  3. One signature: your wallet sends one transaction to a brand-new vault: a SOL transfer plus a Memo instruction patchbay:v1:<fingerprint>. You send your chosen vault buy + 0.03 SOL to create the coin + 0.01 SOL kept for gas.
  4. Create: the server checks your transaction on-chain (signer, amount, memo, not reused), then the vault creates the coin on pump.fun, signed by the vault and the new mint key, with your vault buy as the first buy. The vault then writes patchbay:v1:<mint>:<fingerprint>.
  5. Safety nets: if the create fails, everything in the vault goes back to your wallet automatically. If your funding landed but confirmation timed out, press Retry; a launch left unconfirmed for 2 hours is refunded to you.

Vault

Each coin gets its own fresh Solana keypair. It is the coin's pump.fun creator, so it receives the coin's creator fees. Its secret key is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and used only by the executor, which only signs what the patch prescribes. The vault always keeps 0.01 SOL for gas. Its address, SOL balance, coin balance and stock holdings are on the coin page and on any Solana explorer.

Runs

Every 10 minutes the executor visits each live coin:

  1. Claim: it builds the creator-fee claim and simulates it first, which is free. It only sends the claim when the vault would gain at least 0.002 SOL.
  2. Market: price, market cap and 1h change from DexScreener.
  3. Triggers: each decides whether it fires and with what budget.
  4. Actions: each cable takes its share; SOL actions first, then Burn.

Actions under 0.001 SOL are skipped. Airdrop shares or prizes below 0.00089 SOL (Solana's rent minimum for a new account) to wallets that don't exist yet are skipped, so one tiny share never fails a whole batch.

Receipts

Every claim, buyback, burn, airdrop, random payout, stock buy, creator payment and keep becomes a receipt: kind, amount, the trigger that fired, the text, the time and the transaction signature linking to Solscan. Failed attempts are receipts too, with the reason; their SOL stays in the vault for the next run. Random-holder receipts also carry the seed and the winner.

Verify on Solana /verify/<mint>

  1. Your browser canonicalizes the patch (sorted keys, no whitespace) and hashes it with SHA-256.
  2. It checks the creator's funding memo on Solana matches that hash and was signed by the creator.
  3. It checks the vault's memo binds the same hash to this mint and was signed by the vault.
  4. It links the pump.fun create transaction, which shows the vault is the coin's creator.

The page prints the exact canonical bytes that were hashed, so you can hash them yourself with any SHA-256 tool.

Solana + pump.fun

Coins are standard pump.fun tokens on Solana mainnet and trade anywhere pump.fun coins trade. PATCHBAY uses PumpPortal to build create, buy and creator-fee-claim transactions, Jupiter for stock swaps, DexScreener for market data, and Solana RPC for everything it reads and sends. Every transaction is signed by the coin's own vault.

Webhooks

Get every receipt for a coin pushed to your server the moment it's written, for bots, dashboards or Telegram alerts.

curl -X POST https://patchbay.fun/api/webhooks \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"mint":"<MINT>","url":"https://your.server/hook"}'
→ { "id": "…", "secret": "…" }   (the secret is shown once)

Each delivery is a POST with body {"event":"receipt","mint":…,"receipt":{…}} and header x-patchbay-signature: sha256=<HMAC-SHA256(secret, raw body)>. Check it before trusting the payload. URLs must be public https; up to 20 webhooks per coin; a URL that fails 50 deliveries in a row is removed. Unsubscribe with DELETE /api/webhooks and {"mint","id","secret"}.

Terms

PATCHBAY is software that builds and signs Solana transactions according to rules you choose. By launching a coin you confirm that you have the right to use its name, ticker and image, that you choose its patch knowingly, and that the patch cannot be changed afterwards. Coins launched here are pump.fun tokens and are also subject to pump.fun's terms. PATCHBAY takes no fee and holds no team allocation of any coin. You are responsible for complying with the laws that apply to you.

Risks

  • Markets: memecoin prices move fast and can go to zero. Buybacks, burns and airdrops change supply and distribution; they don't guarantee a price.
  • Fixed rules: a patch runs as written. If a rule turns out not to suit the coin later, it still runs.
  • Vault custody: each vault key is held, encrypted, by the PATCHBAY executor. Its every action is public and verifiable, but the key is not held by the creator.
  • Dependencies: runs rely on Solana, pump.fun, PumpPortal, Jupiter and DexScreener. If one is unavailable, that run's action fails with a receipt and the SOL waits for the next run.
  • Tokenized stocks: xStocks are issued by a third party and follow their issuer's terms.

Privacy

PATCHBAY has no accounts and no email. It stores what a launch needs: the coin's name, ticker, image, links, patch and the creator's public wallet address, plus public on-chain receipts. Your wallet address is remembered in your own browser so you stay connected. Webhook URLs are stored to deliver receipts. There are no ad trackers.
